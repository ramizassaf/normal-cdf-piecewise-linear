Field rule: 50 · 84 · 98 · 100

  1. Remember Φ at z = 0, 1, 2, 3: 0.50 0.84 0.98 1.00
  2. Add slope × distance into the segment: 0.34 on [0,1], 0.14 on [1,2], 0.02 on [2,3].
  3. Negative z: use 1 − Φ̂(|z|). Two-sided coverage: 2Φ̂ − 1.

Worst case: 2.35 pp in Φ at z ≈ 1.45, so up to 4.7 pp in two-sided coverage.

Spreadsheet formula (Model C)

Cell A2 holds z. Returns #N/A outside [−3, 3].

=IF(ABS(A2)>3,NA(),0.5+SIGN(A2)*IF(ABS(A2)<=1,0.34*ABS(A2),IF(ABS(A2)<=2,0.34+0.14*(ABS(A2)-1),0.48+0.02*(ABS(A2)-2))))
